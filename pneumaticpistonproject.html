Machining of Pneumatic Piston

Feb. 2024 | George Brown College – Fundamentals of Machining

Description

As part of the Introduction to Machining course at George Brown College, we learned how to operate various machines, including a milling machine, lathe, and drill press. We then applied our learning to machine and assemble a pneumatic piston from a provided CAD drawing, ensuring to correctly maintain the tolerances as described in the CAD drawing.

Role

I machined the necessary parts for a pneumatic piston using a lathe, milling machine, and drill press, while maintaining the required tolerances to ensure the pieces fit properly. I learned the importance of considering the manufacturability of a design. For example, tolerances must be machinable, some machines have digital measurements which allow for more precise cuts, and some shapes are not practical to machine. Overall, I successfully assembled a working piston and received permission to machine in the University of Toronto’s machine shop.
