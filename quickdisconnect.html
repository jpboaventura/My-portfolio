Quick Disconnect for Liquid Rocket

Apr 2024 - Present | University of Toronto – UTAT Rocketry Division

Project Description

This project involves designing a new Quick Disconnect (QD) system for a liquid rocket, essential for safely starting the engine. The goal is to develop a system that improves safety and optimizes disconnection force. The project covers all aspects of design, testing, and iteration, with the use of CAD for modeling and 3D printing for initial prototypes.

Role

As co-lead of the structures subsystem, I researched potential designs like breakaway couplings and worked with the team to select an appropriate QD. I helped design test components to measure disconnection force and improve the secure mechanism. Moving forward, I will focus on refining the actuation mechanism and testing the system to meet the project’s safety and performance goals.
