Launch Rail and Launch Rail Base Assembly

Jul – Aug. 2024 | University of Toronto Aerospace Team – Rocketry Division

Description

The launch rail and launch rail base was used to secure UTAT’s hybrid rocket, Defiance Mk. IV, at the 2024 Launch Canada competition. The rocket is secured in a vertical position to facilitate filling of the rocket with fuel and guides the rocket in the first few moments of launch, providing stability until aerodynamic forces from the fins are sufficient to maintain its trajectory. The 32 feet of aluminum trusses, along with the launch rail base, which was made from welded steel HSS pieces, were designed to withstand up to 10kN of thrust, while ensuring ease of assembly and reliability.

Role

I collaborated with two other leads in the manufacturing of the launch rail. This involved using a bandsaw to cut steel HSS pieces to the appropriate dimensions (referenced from a CAD), drilling holes using a drill press, MIG welding these HSS pieces together, and cleaning welds with an angle grinder. To enhance durability, I also applied rust-proof paint to protect the base from corrosion and ensure long-term resilience.

Along with two other leads, I aided in the assembly of the launch rail and launch rail base at the 2024 Launch Canada Competition, ensuring stability and an appropriate trajectory for the successful launch of UTAT’s hybrid rocket, which earned the team a 2nd place in the Advanced Category.
