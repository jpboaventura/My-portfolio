ABOUT ME

My passion for aerospace started as a child, watching my dad develop a helicopter simulator. Seeing his work inspired my curiosity for aviation and engineering, which has only grown stronger over the years! I am currently an aerospace engineering student at the University of Toronto with hands-on mechanical design and manufacturing experience. I am grateful to have had the chance to work on several very exciting projects, including Discovery (UTAT's first liquid bipropellant rocket), Defiance Mk. IV (UTAT's hybrid rocket), and the development of a UAV flight test facility! These projects and more are shown below in my portfolio.
