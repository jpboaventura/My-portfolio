Liner Manufacturing

Feb 2024 - Present | University of Toronto Aerospace Team Rocketry Division

Project Description

Liners are critical thermal insulators placed inside the combustion chamber of the rocket to protect it from the intense heat of combustion. This project involves designing and testing a new liner for both hybrid and liquid rockets.

Role

As a co-lead, I manufactured phenolic liners and developed procedures for mold preparation and casting. Designed and 3D-printed mandrels using CAD, researched materials, and created BOMs to streamline production.
